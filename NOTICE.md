# Attribution and external resources

The MIT license covers this release’s original code. It does not relicense datasets, checkpoint weights, upstream files or external vocabulary resources.

- **LAION-CLAP:** https://github.com/LAION-AI/CLAP, commit `1fd4c37df5ffbfcfbad5415c170bc66cf94c9994`. The upstream repository’s top-level license is CC0 1.0; individual files also preserve upstream attributions, including OpenAI CLIP and Microsoft Swin Transformer. Setup downloads the unmodified archive and retains its notices. Model/preprocessing adapters preserve the upstream operations, including the repeatpad and int16 round-trip convention. Checkpoint source and hash are in `assets/protocol.json`; weights are not redistributed in this repository.
- **TextSpan:** Gandelsman, Efros and Steinhardt, *Interpreting CLIP’s Image Representation via Text-Based Decomposition*, ICLR 2024. https://github.com/yossigandelsman/clip_text_span, reference commit `49f70f31bb13437a870ff8de340626b225500a22`. `span.py` is an independent NumPy implementation of raw-variance greedy residualization, with explicit support projection and numerical checks. The frequent-word control uses the first 800 entries of that implementation’s 3,498-word frequency list. No upstream model fork is included.
- **FSD-pruned:** Zhang, Thomaz and Lu, *Transformation of Audio Embeddings into Interpretable, Concept-Based Representations*, IJCNN 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14076, https://osf.io/3cgsu/. The 2,000 entries preserve the supplied `FSD50K_pruned.txt` order; source SHA-256 `2f6f3569f18aa715b32e098a98a2a5372106ecb31805f7ec6a761a16106b43b5`. Counts do not enter fitting. The source project did not expose an explicit vocabulary license at inspection; this vocabulary is not covered by our code license. We make no claim of authorship or ownership of that list.
- **Clotho v2.1:** Drossos, Lipping and Virtanen, *Clotho: an Audio Captioning Dataset*, ICASSP 2020, https://zenodo.org/records/4783391. We use development, validation and evaluation audio as fit data, not its captions. Audio originates from Freesound and has per-recording licenses. The fit manifest retains each recording’s source ID and license. Full audio archives and captions are not redistributed. The website hosts **only CC0** center-window excerpts; `docs/audio/credits.json` records source links, original filenames, original/preview hashes, license URLs and excerpt positions.
- **UrbanSound8K:** Salamon, Jacoby and Bello, *A Dataset and Taxonomy for Urban Sound Research*, ACM Multimedia 2014, https://zenodo.org/records/1203745. Full waveforms are downloaded separately and are not hosted here. The source IDs in the evaluation manifest allow the Clotho overlap exclusion to be checked.
- **RoBERTa:** FacebookAI/roberta-base at revision `e2da8e2f811d1448a5b465c236feacd80ffbac7b`. The checkpoint includes the text weights; only the pinned configuration/tokenizer assets are downloaded separately. Their hashes are recorded in the protocol.

The website’s descriptions are generated research inputs. Mappings and selected-head summaries are qualitative annotations rather than dataset ground truth. Hosted audio selection is deterministic from fitted signed coefficients and the CC0 restriction, not a claim of manual caption verification.
